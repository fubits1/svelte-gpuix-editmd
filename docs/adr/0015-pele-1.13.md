# ADR-0015: pele 1.13.0

## Status

Accepted, 2026-11-05. Amends [ADR-0006](0006-mermaid-via-pele.md) (pele version; replaces its 2026-11-05 amendment).

## Context

editmd shows a Mermaid diagram with a syntax error as mermaid's own message. editmd's picture of the fixture
`r-mermaid-error.md` (the parity kit's `r-mermaid-error-source.png`) shows the line, its context, a dashed `^` under the
failing place and what was expected: `Parse error on line 3:`, `...hart LR  probe -->`, a dashed marker and 13 expected
tokens.

pele 1.10.0 gives an empty context line and a lone `^` for a parse error at the end of the input. pele 1.13.0 gives the
line, its context, a dashed marker and what was expected (`Parse error on line 2:`, `probe -->`, `-------^`,
`Expecting 'NODE_STRING', got 'EOF'`). pele counts the line and words the context and the expected tokens its own way.

## Decision

- Use pele 1.13.0, pinned exactly in `package.json`
- `diagrams.ts` passes pele's message through as it is, cut to its first four non-blank lines as editmd's `errorText`
  does

## Consequences

- A diagram's error message has editmd's shape. Its line number, context and expected tokens are pele's

## Evidence

- `src/app/diagrams.test.ts`: an error at the end of the input gives `Parse error on line 2:`, `A -->`, `-------^`,
  `Expecting 'NODE_STRING', got 'EOF'`
- On pele 1.13.0 the other 39 pages, the measurements of every other fixture and 543 of the UI suite's 545 records were
  unchanged (the measurement behind ADR-0006's 2026-11-05 amendment)
- Socket, 2026-11-05: pele@1.13.0 97, no vulnerabilities, no dependencies
